Our story

Two cities, one eye.

IB Watches began with a simple idea: find good watches in the places that treasure them. Lausanne gives us Swiss precision and provenance. Istanbul gives us a living market of dealers, collectors and craftspeople.

We keep the collection small. We only offer what we would wear ourselves.
